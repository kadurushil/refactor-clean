Release Notes V3.4.9 | 2026-10-06

What's New in Version 3.4.9

Version 3.4.9 delivers major upgrades in radar spatial geometry, staged ADAS collision alerts, multi-source build component tracking, 3D elevation inspection, and temporal synchronization stability.

📐

1. Radar Geometric Engine

drawUtils.js

Corrected non-isometric aspect-ratio distortions while guaranteeing pure orthogonal rectangles.

  • • Eliminated 25m Distortion: Fixed pixel-space rotation bug that stretched 6.2m vehicles to ~25m on anisotropic screen axes.
  • • Rotated Orthogonal Rectangles: Bounding boxes render as clean, true 90° rectangles (p.rect) rotated by visual screen angle with zero parallelogram shearing.
  • • Directional Scale Projection: Computed exact heading scale (lenScale) and cross-range scale (widthScale) preserving true metric meters.
  • • Smooth Covariance Ellipses: Restored native p.ellipse with directional scale mapping and wired ellipse rendering to the close-up zoom panel.
  • • Ego Vehicle Bumper Anchoring: Anchored vehicle front bumper to origin ($Y = 0$) across all radar range slider settings ($40\text{m}\dots200\text{m}$).
Core Visualization Geometric Accuracy

2. ADAS Warnings & POI

radarSketch.js

Staged collision warning halos, lead vehicle identification, and road barrier clutter elimination.

  • • Stage 1 Caution Halo: Dynamic pulsing amber halo around target vehicle for moderate TTC risk ($5\text{s} < \text{TTC} \le 10\text{s}$).
  • • Stage 2 Critical Alert: High-contrast flashing red halo with an exclamation alert triangle badge for imminent collision ($\text{TTC} \le 5\text{s}$).
  • • Lead Vehicle (POI) Highlighting: Lead target marked with high-visibility amber tooltip boundary, leader line, and [LEAD POI] label.
  • • Stationary Clutter Suppression: Dimension boxes for stationary objects are restricted exclusively to the active lead vehicle within the driving corridor.
  • • Timeline Speed Graph Bands: Color-coded warning bands on the speed graph with 5px min-width and top accent notches.

fileLoader.js

Container-level frame alignment and asynchronous race condition protection.

  • • Hard Reload (Ctrl+Shift+R) Protection: Guarded speedGraphSketch buffer initialization against asynchronous startup race conditions.

6. VSync & Display Fluidity

radarSketch.js

Fluid rendering pipeline tuned for modern high-refresh hardware displays.

  • • Native Display VSync: Increased framerate ceiling to p.frameRate(240) to eliminate micro-judder on 75Hz, 120Hz, and 144Hz monitors.
  • • Continuous Loop Animations: Active loop runs live while paused so pulsing warning halos and HUD badges animate smoothly.
  • • Rolling-Window FPS Engine: Replaced noisy instantaneous FPS calculation with a 500ms rolling-window smoothed measurement.
